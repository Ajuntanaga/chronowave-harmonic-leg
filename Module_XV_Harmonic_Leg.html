XV The harmonic leg

One qubit, strung between τ|0⟩ and t|1⟩

From the canvas panels: a temporal Bloch sphere whose poles are τ|0⟩ (Root; 1) and t|1⟩ at 180°. Ground flip inverts the sphere and the labels with it: τ|1⟩ above, t|0⟩ below. The octave is not a pole at all — it is τ|0⟩′, the τ pole reached again at 360°, and τ|0⟩″ at 720° closes the double cover. The ladder runs 720° at 30° per semitone (π/6 semitone, π/3 wholetone), the yield flipping −i to +i on the second cover. |En⟩m ≠ |En⟩n±m. The superposition plane is not flat: it is a planar volume. Its top face carries one phase, az₁ = φτ, and holds the modal-invariant scale — every interval not in the main set, plus the same root position. Its bottom face carries the second phase, az₂ = φt, and holds the anti-scale — the complement joined with the root and the tritone. Each hemisphere rotates with its own localized gate, or both together under Tgbl for unison motion, as more normal. Drag the sphere to move the state.

The stepwise sequence is interval motion: W H H H H H W W H, wholetones π/3 and semitones π/6 summing to 2π — Tgbl transforms both hemispheres as if unified. The localized T operators rotate each hemisphere independently, forward or backward: anti-transpositionals about the superposition plane. That plane has depth — each azimuth there is a pair. Its top rim is the modal-invariant scaling: always beginning from the same initial state (Root; 1) and processing through each interval not in the main set. Its bottom rim is the anti-scale: the same complement, without respect to the root. Localization without variance permits a maximum unification of two positional types at a time — θ-positional with az₁, or az₁ with az₂; a third coincidence is auto-broken by a semitone. The centripetal cross-section (top right) carries the three point-nodes: state, az₁, az₂. Voice: the amplitudes weight two oscillators — root at the τ pole, √2 at the t pole — and crossing 360° onto cover II lifts both an octave: τ|0⟩′, the root returned primed. The rims read the main set live from Module IV: change the basis there and the modal-invariant and anti-scale faces re-derive here.
